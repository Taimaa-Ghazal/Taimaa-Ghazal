# Hi, I'm Taimaa Ghazal

Mechatronics engineer and robotics instructor. I work on embedded systems, automation and mechanical design, with an interest in assistive technology and environmental robotics.

## What I work with

- **Programming:** Python, C++, MATLAB
- **Embedded systems:** Arduino, ESP32, IMU sensors, RFID and motor control
- **Design and automation:** SOLIDWORKS, CAD, circuit prototyping, PLC programming and Grafcet
- **Computer vision and IoT:** OpenCV, connected devices and n8n

## Selected projects

- **[Mangrove Pulse](https://github.com/Salim-Ammar/mangrove-pulse)** — Team leader and concept development lead for a satellite-guided mangrove inspection project in the 813 Challenge. Our PoC connects Earth-observation analysis to a proposed amphibious drone.
- **Smart Revolving Pyramid Hotel** — Graduation project combining a motor-driven rotating structure with IoT controls, a Python interface, facial recognition and RFID access.
- **Hand-Gesture Smart Chair** — A mobility prototype that translates IMU-based hand gestures into motor commands.
- **PET Recycling Machine** — A desktop robotic project for turning plastic bottles into 3D-printer filament; a top-10 finalist in the Syrian Technocrats Competition.

## Teaching and background

I teach embedded systems and robotics at the Syrian Commission for Excellence and Creativity, and previously trained students at the Syrian Computer Society. My work includes hands-on prototyping, hardware debugging and mentoring competition teams.

BSc in Mechatronics Engineering, University of Latakia.
